2026 Assessment Overview

The current school handbook publishes ongoing assessment components, not separately numbered formal tasks.

  • Practical: ongoing, 60% — handbook outcomes IND4-1, IND4-3, IND4-4 and IND4-7.
  • Safety: ongoing, 20% — handbook outcomes IND4-1 and IND4-6.
  • Knowledge: ongoing, 20% — handbook outcomes IND4-2, IND4-5, IND4-8 and IND4-10.

Outcome check required: the handbook’s IND4 mapping conflicts with the rebuilt site’s new-syllabus TE4 mapping. The teacher must confirm the approved local 2026 mapping before student issue.
