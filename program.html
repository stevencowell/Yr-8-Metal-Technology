Hose Reel Holder program summary

This is a 20-week, 50-period introductory unit contributing to a 100-hour Industrial Technology – Metal elective. Students attend 5 × 60-minute periods per fortnight, averaging 2.5 hours per week.

Five-module sequence

  1. Weeks 1–4: Project purpose and workshop readiness
  2. Weeks 5–8: Design, plans and accurate marking
  3. Weeks 9–12: Cut, shape and drill under control
  4. Weeks 13–16: Join, assemble and protect
  5. Weeks 17–20: Test, evaluate and present evidence

The detailed 50-period sequence is available through Teacher Resources. Additional study is required to complete the school's named 100-hour elective.
