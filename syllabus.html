Industrial Technology 7–10 Syllabus (2025)

The Hose Reel Holder is a Year 8 Stage 4 introductory unit contributing to a 100-hour Industrial Technology – Metal elective. The unit uses approved early adoption for 2027.

Stage 4 adjustment

Stage 5 INT5 outcomes are adjusted for Stage 4 under syllabus advice INT4-ADJ-01. The teaching program maps only the outcomes and content supported by the actual unit; the site does not claim whole-syllabus coverage.

  • INT5-SAF-01 risk management and safe work practices
  • INT5-COM-01 communication of ideas and processes
  • INT5-ENV-01 impacts of industrial activities and practices
  • INT5-DES-01 design, project management and technical skills
  • INT5-USE-01 selection and justification of materials, tools and equipment
  • INT5-EVL-01 evaluation of product qualities
  • INT5-INP-01 industrial processes in production contexts
  • INT5-PRO-01 practical project-production skills
