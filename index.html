The new syllabus is implemented from 2026. This project can provide opportunities to develop the outcomes below; final school programming and assessment mapping remain teacher decisions.

  • TE4-SDP-01 sustainability, design and production

  • TE4-PPM-01 planning, management and production
  • TE4-DES-01 communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
  • TE4-SAF-01 safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes
  • TE4-DIG-01 safe digital interaction
  • TE4-DIG-02 data and digital systems in design and production
