Project brief

State the need, intended user and project purpose in your own words.

Users and constraints

Identify requirements, preferences and constraints. Exact details come from the issued plan.

WHS and risk controls

Record hazards, stronger controls, PPE and permission points for planned stages.

Research

Summarise useful existing solutions or metalwork practices and cite sources.

Ideas and decisions

Add concept evidence and explain why the approved direction meets the brief.

Working drawing evidence

Refer to the teacher-issued drawing. Record what views, notes and checks guided production.

Materials and tools

Record teacher-specified materials and approved tools, with reasons where known.

Production plan

List the planned sequence, checkpoints and teacher demonstrations required.

Mark, cut and form

Add your own evidence photos or captions showing accuracy and safe workholding.

Join and finish

Explain trial fitting, the approved joint and corrosion-protection preparation.

Testing and problem solving

Record teacher-approved tests, results, problems, decisions and corrections.

Final evaluation

Judge the result with specific evidence and propose one realistic improvement.

Keep evidence specific, honest and connected to your own project work.
